# 4. Views Overview

This chapter covers the three non-calendar task views. Calendar views are covered in their own chapter ([5. Calendar Views](04-calendar-views.md)), and the Habit Tracker module in [6. Habit Tracker](05-habit-tracker.md).

## Overview view

A dashboard-style summary of your tasks, meant to be the first thing you check. It includes:

- Key metrics and statistics about your tasks (totals, completion, overdue count, etc.).
- Quick access to upcoming deadlines.
- A breakdown of tasks by project/folder.
- Overdue tasks highlighted for visibility.
- Customizable widgets.

## List view

A flexible, detailed list of tasks, best for working through a filtered subset of your vault's tasks one by one.

- Customizable columns.
- Advanced filtering by date, project, priority, and tags.
- Grouping by date, folder, status, or custom attributes, with collapsible groups.
- Click a task to edit it inline or in the modal (see [8. Gestures and Interactions](07-gestures.md)).
- Bulk actions for multiple tasks at once.

## Table view

A spreadsheet-style grid, best when you want to scan, sort, and compare many tasks at a glance.

- Customizable columns and layout.
- Sortable and resizable columns — click a column header to sort, click again to reverse.
- Quick entry and editing of task attributes directly in the grid.
- Status and priority cues, compact date/tag styling, conditional formatting.
- CSV export.
- Search, filters, and sort order are remembered when you return from editing a task or switch views; column widths adapt to the rows visible after filtering.
